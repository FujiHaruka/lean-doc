  235,185 + 16,040 = 251,225 and 235,185 + 2,891 + 3,477 = 241,553: the two
  populations are fully accounted for.

What the two one-sided buckets are (investigated, not waved away):

  * 16,040 in the .lidx and not in the oracle — 16,038 of them have a *parent*
    name that IS in the oracle: they are structure constructors (`.mk`, 3,493)
    and structure field projections (`name` 97, `type` 62, `ref` 56, …), which
    doc-gen4 renders inside the parent structure's decl div instead of giving
    each one a `<div class="decl" id=…>` of its own. The oracle mines those
    divs, so it is silent about them — not a disagreement.
    The remaining 2 are `Qq.Impl.mkLambdaQ` and `Qq.Impl.withLetHave`
    (Qq/MatchImpl.lean:51 and Qq/Match.lean:99, real `def`s). doc-gen4's
    Qq/MatchImpl.html carries 9 decl divs and stops at `mkLambda'`/`mkLet'`
    (lines 45-49); the two declarations after them are on no page in the tree.
    That is a gap in the oracle, not in the .lidx.
  * 2,891 in the oracle and not in the .lidx — 2,890 are `Lake.*` and the last
    one is `Opaque.mk`, which is Lake's (Lake/Util/Opaque.html), not core's.
    **Lake is not in the target's import closure**; doc-gen4's own build
    documents it because doc-gen4 imports Lake. Nothing the extraction saw.

Name escaping — the difference the brief expected, and its size:

  * `.lidx` writes `Name.toString` (escaping on): 795 names carry guillemets,
    and all 795 are in the oracle with the same guillemets.
  * the oracle's names come from doc-gen4's HTML `id=`, so `<` `>` `&` arrive as
    entities: 65 such names, e.g.
        oracle  Cardinal.«term_^&lt;_»
        .lidx   Cardinal.«term_^<_»
    Undoing the HTML escape (and only that) reconciles all 65, with 0 name
    collisions over the whole 241,553-entry oracle. It is the *only* spelling
    difference between the two sides: nothing else needed normalising.
