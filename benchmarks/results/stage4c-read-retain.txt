One fresh process per measurement, 7 each. The ONLY difference between the two
series is whether the parsed module object stays reachable after it is counted.
read-ir.ts drops it; render.ts keeps it, because a renderer needs the whole IR.

| spread (min-max) | 0.0966-0.0983 | 0.1162-0.1187 |

Retaining the parsed IR costs **+0.0194 s (+20%)**, which is the whole of the gap between
`benchmarks/tools/read-ir.ts`'s 0.105 s (fresh process, drops each module) and
`render.ts`'s 0.130 s read-IR phase. Neither number is wrong; they measure
different consumers. **Quote 0.100 s only for a consumer that streams.**
