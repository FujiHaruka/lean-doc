## H(mu; X | Y) never appears -- and that is correct

The notation is declared over Shannon.condEntropy, which is a [reducible] alias of
MeasureFano.condEntropy. Elaborated terms therefore hold MeasureFano.condEntropy,
which the notation3 delaborator does not match, so the conditional-entropy notation
can never fire. 34 declarations mention condEntropy in their type; all of them print
MeasureFano.condEntropy ... in both modes, and doc-gen4 prints the same. This is a
property of how the alias was declared, not of the extractor.
